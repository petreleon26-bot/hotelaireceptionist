Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before testing HotelAIReceptionist.

Does the AI Receptionist replace my reception staff?

No. It is designed to reduce repetitive guest enquiries and support your team, allowing staff to focus on situations that require personal attention.

Can it answer guests in different languages?

Yes. The AI can communicate with international guests in multiple languages.

Where does the AI get its information?

The AI is configured using information supplied by your property, including your website, FAQ, policies, services and other approved content.

Can I update the information later?

Yes. Your knowledge base can be updated when opening hours, services, policies or other hotel information changes.

Does it work on mobile devices?

Yes. The website chatbot is designed to work on desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

Can it connect to WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp AI Receptionist functionality is available with our Premium and customized solutions.

Can it integrate with our PMS or booking system?

Custom integrations can be evaluated depending on your PMS, booking engine or channel manager and the integration options they provide.

Do I have to pay before testing it?

No. We first configure your AI Receptionist and provide a two-week pilot. Payment is requested only after you approve the pilot and decide to continue.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We handle the initial configuration and provide the information needed to integrate the chatbot into your website.

Can you create a customized solution?

Yes. Enterprise solutions are available for hotel groups, resorts, multi-property businesses and projects requiring custom integrations.
